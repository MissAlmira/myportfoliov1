AI Evaluation & Transcription

Fictional evaluation examples illustrating H2H, SxS, and audio transcription workflows.
PROMPT DEMO

"Write a polite, 2-sentence response denying a refund request due to expired policy (30 days limit)."

Model A Response Failed Criteria

"We cannot refund your item because 30 days have passed. You should check our refund policy before buying products on our website."

• Tone: Blunt and slightly accusatory.
• Constraint Check: 2 sentences, but lacks politeness guidelines.
Model B Response Winner (Slightly Better)

"Thank you for reaching out, but unfortunately, we are unable to process a refund as your order exceeds our 30-day return window. We truly appreciate your understanding and are happy to help with any other questions."

• Tone: Professional, courteous, empathetic.
• Constraint Check: Exactly 2 sentences, full rubric compliance.
Justification / Rating Rationale

Model B selected as winner. Model B followed both length constraints and tone guidelines effectively. Model A was unnecessarily hostile towards the user.

RUBRIC-BASED AUDIT

Prompt: "Summarize the steps to reset a router password in bullet points."

Response Output 1

To reset your router: 1. Hold reset button for 10 sec. 2. Log into 192.168.1.1. 3. Enter default admin credentials.

Rating: Satisfactory (Used numbered list instead of bullet points requested).
Response Output 2
  • Locate physical reset pinhole button.
  • Press and hold for 10–15 seconds.
  • Navigate to default gateway IP in browser.
  • Update credentials in Admin tab.
Rating: Excellent (Followed formatting constraints strictly).
